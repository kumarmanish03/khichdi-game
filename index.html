WELCOME TO THE KITCHEN

One pot.
Everyone fed.

Your mission is to fit the whole recipe into the pot, cook it, and serve every guest.

  1. Pack: tap + Add for a piece or scoop, or All to pour that ingredient. Finish potatoes → chunky veggies → peas → dal → rice. A wrong pour blocks the batch until you undo or restart.
  2. Cook: only a correctly packed recipe can cook. Stir three times; stirring never creates extra space or fixes a wrong order.
  3. Serve: ladle out the khichdi and watch the happy bowls appear!

Got a potato traffic jam? Undo or start with a fresh pot. This is a playful packing puzzle; quantities are game tokens.
